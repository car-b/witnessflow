## Supplementary Table S1

**Supplementary Table 1 | Datasets, sample inclusion and cell accounting (related to Figs. 1e, 3d, 4a and 4e and Supplementary Fig. 1a).**

Generated by `figures/scripts/table_S1.py` from frozen inputs and existing deterministic restores (`figures/derive/sf1_ledger.py`: same loaders, split seeds, thresholds and gates as the frozen replay). Every restored count is asserted against a frozen result table. No experiment, test or resampling was added.

**Files** (61 rows; one raw FCS file per row). Levine-32: 1 file holding 2 individuals (265,627 events). WINGMEN: 54 files (27 pre-treatment, 27 post-treatment). CytoNorm: 6 files (3 donors × 2 runs, 1,000 events each). 41 files were not used: 14 pre-treatment files below the protocol minimum of 30,000 events (the largest is PreWN08 with 28,060) and all 27 post-treatment files (outside the protocol file pattern). The 61 rows are therefore not 61 analysed samples. `events_raw` is the `$TOT` keyword; `instrument_keyword` transcribes `$CYT`/`$CYTSN` from the FCS TEXT segment. Source fields are filled only from recorded evidence. WINGMEN: figshare 30174547 (doi 10.6084/m9.figshare.30174547.v1, CC BY 4.0, PBMC, NCT05110495), from the frozen protocol. Levine-32: the recorded benchmark-repository URL. CytoNorm: the package's example-data folder. Original accessions, licences, tissues or instrument keywords that are not recorded are marked `not_recorded`; a software repository's code licence is not used as a data licence. The SHA256 of every file equals the original freeze manifest.

**Splits** (15 rows = 2 Levine-32 individuals + 13 WINGMEN donors). Cells are split at random within each individual or sample into calibration, initial, follow-up and reference sets (20/40/20/20%; seeds 20261008 and 20261009), after the event filter (Levine-32: none; WINGMEN: dead-cell filter). Follow-up totals are 53,125 (Levine-32) and 159,264 (WINGMEN); initial totals are 106,250 and 318,526. For WINGMEN, `n_followup` equals N_d of the per-donor replay: all follow-up cells of the donor, not a parent-population count.

**Parents** (10 rows = 2 datasets × 5 parent populations). Parent counts per split and the parent share π of the initial split (denominator: all initial cells). Parents are defined by backbone gates and need not be disjoint: in Levine-32, the gate definitions do not exclude HSPC from Mono or NK (`may_overlap_with`). Parents also do not cover all cells, so parent counts must not be summed to obtain total cells.

**Donor_parent** (26 rows = 13 WINGMEN donors × CD4 T and CD8 T). Per-donor parent counts in the follow-up and reference splits, N_d, and the inclusion rule of the exploratory per-donor replay (≥ 500 parent cells in both splits). 25 of 26 combinations are included; CD8 T of donor 6 (410 and 384 cells) is excluded.

**RareB_sampling** (3 rows). B-cell thinning in the Levine-32 rare-population stress protocols to 1%, 0.3% and 0.1% of all cells (initial and follow-up splits only, seed 11). This keeps 842, 251 and 83 B cells in the initial split; the reference split (11,199 B cells) is untouched.

**Preprocessing** (75 rows; one dataset × parameter). Transform, event filter, split, threshold rule, backbone, initial and follow-up designs and claim goal, transcribed from the protocol JSON files. The WINGMEN marker naming comes from the frozen loader, and the CytoNorm constants from `src/real_batch.py` (CytoNorm has no protocol). The WINGMEN e3 values are listed separately as exploratory replay tolerances, not as the protocol ε.

## Supplementary Table S2

**Supplementary Table 2 | Marker thresholds, gating definitions and evaluated claims (related to Figs. 1b, 1d, 1e, 2a, 2d and 4a and Supplementary Figs. 1b, 1d and 2c).**

Generated by `figures/scripts/table_S2.py` from frozen inputs only. Thresholds were not refitted, and no biological annotation or significance judgement was added.

**Markers** (69 rows = 32 Levine-32 + 37 WINGMEN). One row per marker with:

- the FCS parameter number, plus `channel` (= `$PnN`) and `stain_raw` (= `$PnS`) as written in the FCS TEXT segment;
- the marker's role (backbone 8 + 8; candidate 20 + 26);
- the threshold diagnostics of the protocol rule, on the asinh(x/5) scale.

A marker is reliable if Ashman's D ≥ 1.5 and the upper mixture weight ≥ 0.03. Seven markers fail, 4 + 3: Levine-32 CD235ab, Flt3, CD41 and CD61; WINGMEN CTLA4, CD123 and TIGIT. WINGMEN channels are metal channels (e.g. CD4: `$P22N` = Nd145Di, `$P22S` = 145Nd_CD4), and the parameter map is identical in all 54 files. For Levine-32, `$PnN` already holds the marker name and `$PnS` is empty: the frozen file provides no isotope mapping, and none is inferred. `design_point_seed0` is each candidate's design point under the seed-0 mapping used by the frozen replays. Levine-32 thresholds under the superseded v1 rule are shown for reference only.

**Claims** (27 rows = 12 Levine-32 + 15 WINGMEN). The protocol claims, each with its parent, three markers, positive/negative pattern, protocol rationale text, reliability and evaluation status. Each row also gives the reference frequency (reference split; ε = 0.02) with its 95% Clopper–Pearson interval and n_ref. `protocol_claim_id` is the protocol's own numbering and differs from the paper claims C1–C13 (Supplementary Table 7). The initial audit evaluates all 12 + 15 claims (120 + 150 records). This includes H3 (Flt3) and D2 (TIGIT), whose markers fail the reliability rule; both rows carry a warning. The protocol-specified follow-up endpoint targets only the 11 + 14 reliable-marker claims. The reference half-width exceeds ε only for M2 (0.021).

**Candidate_marker_tube_incidence** (201 rows = 130 + 71 tubes). 0/1 incidence of candidate markers in the two classical designs:

- S26 = S(3,6,26), the La Jolla C(26,6,3) covering: 130 tubes × 26 WINGMEN candidates.
- C20 = the La Jolla C(20,6,3) covering: 71 tubes × 20 Levine-32 candidates.

Each row has six 1s; the indicator columns of the other design are empty (not applicable). Every tube also carries the 8 backbone markers of its dataset, which are implied and not shown. The point-to-marker mapping and tube numbering reproduce `budget.ljcr_stack(seed = 0)` of the frozen replays (0-based `tube_index`; Tube 73 = index 72 contains CCR7, CD27 and CD45RA). The matrix shows the computational co-measurement arrangement only. It is not evidence of physical split-tube staining, panel performance or channel pairing.

## Supplementary Table S3

**Supplementary Table 3 | Design certificates, historical upper-bound comparisons and unsuccessful searches (related to Figs. 1a and 2a–d and Supplementary Fig. 3a, b, d).**

Generated by `figures/scripts/table_S3.py` from frozen files by renaming, joins, differences and enumeration of saved block tables only; no search and no new test was run. Search provenance: see Methods.

**Improved_bounds** (44 rows). Selected feasible upper-bound improvements relative to historical snapshots. One row per covering parameter cell C(v,k,t) in which the computational search produced a feasible covering with fewer blocks than the historical online covering table. Screen: 2 ≤ t ≤ 4, v ≤ 120, k ≤ 40 (t = 3: 20 cells; t = 4: 24; none at t = 2). Only improved cells are listed; the complete search grid is outside the frozen scope, so no success rate can be derived and unlisted cells are not "untried". `search_upper_bound` is the search value. `historical_online_upper_bound` is the online covering table in the snapshot of 2026-10-04 (it may have changed since). `historical_ljcr_upper_bound` is the La Jolla Covering Repository v1.2 frozen snapshot, listed in its own column; the two historical values differ in 5 cells. `saved_vs_online` and `saved_vs_ljcr` are the differences in blocks. `recorded_lower_bound` is the La Jolla lower bound as stored in the scan table; it was not recomputed. `search_round` gives the round(s) of computational search, R1–R4, that produced the value; the generating run of a cell is not identified in the released files (the technique-level lineage with run identifiers is in the Source Data of Supplementary Fig. 3a). `derived_by_point_deletion` marks the 2 cells obtained by deleting points from a larger covering. Every value was re-checked by independent code (`crosscheck.tsv`): the block count equals the claimed value and no t-subset is uncovered. The median saving of 1 block, the total of 172 blocks and the 27 cells saving exactly 1 block hold for these 44 cells only. Two other rows are excluded from these statistics. One is the classical reference C(26,6,3) = 130, the extra row of the Supplementary Fig. 3a Source Data: the Steiner system S(3,6,26), equal to the Schönheim bound and not an improvement. The other is the crosscheck cell C(33,20,5) = 42, which has t = 5 and lies outside the screen. All search values are feasible upper bounds. They are not proved optima and are not claimed as current best values, and none has been submitted. Random-design comparisons and the `ratio_full` column of the scan table are not included.

**Designs** (4 rows) and **Blocks** (896 rows = 130 + 71 + 125 + 570). These are the four designs used in the WitnessFlow replays:

- S26: S(3,6,26), the La Jolla C(26,6,3) covering (WINGMEN).
- C20: the La Jolla C(20,6,3) covering, 71 blocks (Levine-32).
- OA: the 125 quadratic polynomials over F5. This is a strength-3 orthogonal array under the artificial 5-family constraint, with point = 5 × family + slot and block size 5.
- PSL: one PSL(2,19) orbit on 6-subsets, a 3-(20,6,10) design with 570 blocks.

Blocks lists design point labels in source order (0-based `block_index`; the La Jolla blocks are read with sqlite3 from the frozen `base_ljcr.db`). The point-to-marker assignment differs between experiments (Designs: `point_to_marker_mapping`); the seed-0 tube–marker incidence is in Supplementary Table 2. λ is the number of blocks containing a target 3-subset, counted over every target:

| Design | Targets | λ |
|---|---|---|
| S26 | all 2,600 triples | λ = 1 |
| C20 | all 1,140 triples | λ ≥ 1 (range 1–6) |
| OA | the 1,250 cross-family triples only | λ = 1 |
| PSL | all 1,140 triples | λ = 10 |

**verify_designs.py.** This script runs on its own: it uses only the Python standard library and no project code, and it reads only `tsv/S3_Blocks.tsv`. The four design specifications are written into the script: block count × size, target set, pass condition and the OA family rule. It checks the following:

- design presence;
- block count and block size;
- point range;
- repeated points and repeated blocks;
- λ over the stated target set (multiplicity histograms include zero).

It prints PASS or FAIL with reasons for each design and exits with a non-zero code on any failure; all four designs pass. The script verifies the combinatorial properties of these four designs only. It does not cover the 44 improved-bound certificates and does not verify automorphism groups, isomorphism identity or optimality.

**Search_runs** (20 rows). N1: the 20 saved runs that searched for a 141-block 2-fold covering of 20 points with blocks of 6. Two methods were run, annealing and breakout, with budgets of 600 s (seeds 0–4) and 1800 s (seeds 10–14); these are separate runs, not continuations. For each saved block table, the deficiency Σ max(0, 2 − λ), λ_min and the numbers of triples with λ = 0 and λ = 1 were recomputed; all equal the stored values. No run found a certificate (0/20). The final deficiency was 2–7, with no triple left at λ = 0. The lower bound is 117 (Schönheim). A feasible 142-block design is two stacked C(20,6,3) copies (λ_min = 2). Not found is not the same as impossible, and 142 is not proved optimal.

**Verification_scope** (8 rows). For each check, what it covers and what it does not. This includes the two "45th rows" and why both are excluded from the 44-cell statistics.

## Supplementary Table S4

**Supplementary Table 4 | Complete audit results and protocol-specified follow-up endpoints (related to Figs. 1e, 4a and 4e and Supplementary Figs. 1c, 2c and 3e).**

Generated by `figures/scripts/table_S4.py` from the 12 frozen `initial_audit.tsv` and `followup.tsv` files, the two `e5g_threshold_audit.tsv` files and the protocol JSON files. Only renaming, joins and one deterministic aggregation were applied; no test, interval, optimisation or LP was rerun. Record counts are asserted against the files. The exploratory `q1_*` columns of the initial-audit files are not included. `protocol_claim_id` is the protocol's own numbering (WINGMEN uses C1–C5, D1–D4, …) and differs from the paper claims C1–C13 (Supplementary Table 7).

**Initial_main** (270 rows = 120 Levine-32 + 150 WINGMEN; protocol × claim × initial replicate, 10 replicates). Every claim of the two main protocols, including H3 and D2, whose markers fail the reliability rule (flagged; `followup_claim_status` = excluded). Each record gives:

- the LP confidence bound and the identification bound for the claim frequency (the identification bound fixes every observed event margin, including the full-pattern event where a direct witness exists, at its value under the EM fit below, without sampling allowance);
- the point estimates: the EM fit, column `maxent` (claim frequency under the joint distribution fitted by EM maximum likelihood from a uniform initialisation; the field name `maxent` is historical), conditional independence and kNN imputation;
- the direct estimate with its 95% Clopper–Pearson interval where a tube measured all three markers (no direct witness in 116/120 and 145/150 records);
- the per-tube consistency fields, the K = 4 and K = 8 micro-strata sensitivities, and the reference frequency with its interval.

Missing fields carry a status column. The K = 8 sensitivity is not applicable for WINGMEN (the protocol specifies K = 4 only). The per-tube check is not applicable for WINGMEN, because no two initial tubes share a claim marker (not checkable, which is not a pass); it was not computed in the Levine-32 main file.

**Initial_stress** (840 rows = 3 rare-B settings × 120 + 4 tube-shift settings × 120). The seven Levine-32 stress protocols. Rare-B: B cells thinned to 1%, 0.3% or 0.1% of all cells in the initial and follow-up splits. Tube shift: the markers measured in initial tube 0 (all markers or non-backbone markers only) shifted by +0.5 or −0.5 asinh units, plus a zero-shift control on the same code path. These are simulated perturbations, not real batch data. With the +0.5 shift on all markers, the stratified confidence LP is infeasible (incompatible observed margins) in 86 (K = 4) and 87 (K = 8) of the 120 records. In the tube-shift replays the K = 4/8 stratum assignments were computed from the unshifted backbone values and parent gates and cached for reuse, so cells that enter a parent gate only after the shift are assigned the default stratum 0; this infeasibility may therefore partly reflect the stratum assignment rather than the shift itself. The unstratified (K = 1) bounds are not affected. No confidence bound exists there (`conf_K4_status`/`conf_K8_status` = not_applicable), and the stored `ref_in` value (False, from a comparison with a missing bound) is blanked. The identification widths (`ident_w_K4/K8`) come from a separate LP in which every observed event margin is fixed at its value under the EM-fitted joint distribution, without sampling allowance; they are finite in every record and have their own status columns.

**Followup_main** (17,500 rows = 7,700 Levine-32 + 9,800 WINGMEN) and **Followup_stress** (53,900 rows = 7 × 7,700). The protocol-specified follow-up endpoint, one row per evaluated claim × initial replicate × r × method × follow-up replicate (`frep`). Definitions:

- r is the number of added follow-up tubes (1, 2, 3, 4, 6; there is no r = 5). The endpoint pools initial and follow-up evidence. Only the 11 (Levine-32) and 14 (WINGMEN) reliable-marker claims are evaluated. This reliability restriction applies to the evaluated claims and their target triples; the follow-up designs draw tube markers from all non-backbone markers, including those failing the reliability rule.
- `resolved`: a direct witness exists and the 95% Clopper–Pearson half-width of the direct estimate is ≤ the protocol ε (0.02).
- `resolved_and_ref_overlap` (original field `correct`): resolved, and the direct interval intersects the reference interval. It is not an accuracy.
- `weighted_cov` is the sum of the weights of the covered target triples, not a 0–1 coverage fraction.
- `n_targets` is the number of target triples used to design the follow-up for that initial replicate. It is not the denominator of the 11/14 evaluated claims.

random and uniform_LJCR have 5 follow-up replicates per initial replicate and the other four methods 1, so record counts differ between methods. uniform_LJCR draws r blocks of the La Jolla covering table and is not a complete classical covering. The ilp method ran with a 20 s solver time limit, so optimality was not guaranteed. The endpoint is called protocol-specified, not pre-registered, because levine32_v2 was revised after unblinding.

**Followup_summary** (360 rows = 12 protocols × 6 methods × 5 values of r; 60 main). Each method is first averaged over its follow-up replicates within an initial replicate, then over the initial replicates (10; 2 for levine32_v1). The initial replicates are the independent units; more records are not more independent evidence. `resolved_fraction` and `resolved_and_ref_overlap_fraction` use the evaluated claims as denominator. The per-replicate minimum and maximum are a descriptive range, not an interval. The conditional fraction (overlap among resolved claims) lists its denominator twice, as raw resolved records and in per-frep units. The pooled rates equal the follow-up register behind Supplementary Fig. 3e. Superseded protocols appear with `analysis_status` = superseded and are not used in comparisons.

**Legacy_initial** (174 initial-audit records) and **Legacy_followup** (10,520 follow-up records). The superseded settings, each with its `superseded_reason`:

- levine32_v1: superseded threshold rule; quick run with 2 initial replicates (24 initial-audit / 720 follow-up records).
- levine32_v2_bb4 and levine32_v2_bb6: reduced-backbone stress runs that predate the random-number separation fix and the per-tube check (50 / 3,500 and 100 / 6,300 records).

These records are kept for provenance only and are not mixed into current summaries, means or rankings.

**Threshold_audit** (125 rows = 55 Levine-32 + 70 WINGMEN = (11 + 14) reliable claims × 5 threshold offsets). Every marker threshold is shifted by −0.25 to +0.25 asinh units, and the first initial design is audited at each offset. Columns: the LP confidence bound, the reference frequency recomputed at that offset, whether the bound contains it (125/125), the direct cells, and the block-kNN imputed frequency with its absolute error.

## Supplementary Table S5

**Supplementary Table 5 | Complete design replays, requirement ablations and target-level evidence ledgers (related to Figs. 2e, 3b–d and 5a–e and Supplementary Figs. 2a, 2b, 2e and 3c).**

Generated by `figures/scripts/table_S5.py` from the frozen replay tables and ledgers. Only renaming, joins and deterministic differences were applied; no optimiser, LP, replay or simulation was rerun, and no test or interval was added. Settings that a frozen file does not store are filled from the frozen generating code; the script asserts the code text, and `fill_note` marks the filled rows.

- **Denominators.** `n_target_triples` is the denominator of `covered`, `half_ok` and `err_ok`. It is all 3-subsets of the reliable candidate markers: 1,140 for Levine-32 (20 candidates) and 2,600 for WINGMEN (26 candidates). In OA_runs only, it is the 1,250 cross-family triples. The two universes must not be mixed.
- **Tolerances.** ε in this table is a replay tolerance chosen per module for displaying the mechanism (for e5b, chosen after earlier results). In Donor_runs it is the donor-adaptive ε_d. Neither is the protocol ε of Supplementary Table 4. `plan_eps` is empty where λ_min = 0 (stored as infinite).
- **Repeated registrations.** `eval_id` identifies one design evaluation (design × cell allocation × tolerance × threshold offset). Several frozen files record the same evaluation with identical metrics (asserted): the classical stack in e5f and e5i and across initial replicates; e5f hybrid_lp and hybrid_worst and the e5i true and worst-case requirements with optimiser seed 4; and the offset-0 rows of Threshold_design and Node_runs. `n_registrations` counts these rows. `baseline_id` names the evaluation of the classical stack (OA_runs: OA_quadratic) with the same cells and tolerance. Repeated rows, and rows that cite the same baseline, are not independent evidence. All replicate levels are randomisation replicates, not biological replicates.

**Node_runs** (3,240 rows = 1,560 Levine-32 + 1,540 WINGMEN + 140 r = 180 supplement). Every saved node-grid evaluation: parent (Levine-32 T and B; WINGMEN CD4 T and CD8 T) × r (11 nodes per dataset, plus r = 180 for WINGMEN) × design × 10 cell allocations. The designs are the classical stack, random and balanced random (3 seeds each), and for Levine-32 the PSL 3-design at r = 570. The r = 180 file stores no dataset, ε or planning scale. ε is filled from the frozen setting (CD4 T 0.05, CD8 T 0.08), and `eps_star` is marked not saved.

**Donor_runs** (900 rows = 25 donor–parent combinations × 6 nodes × 2 designs × 3 allocations). The exploratory per-donor replay of WINGMEN, which uses each donor's own follow-up and reference cells; the grid used r = 180 instead of the specified 195. Rows give N_d (all follow-up cells of the donor, not parent cells) and ε_d = 1.1 × the donor's planning scale.

**Tube_split** (20 rows = 2 parents × 10 allocations). The 130-tube classical design compared with the same cells split at random into 260 tubes with identical panels. The pooled per-triple counts are identical in all 20 rows (maximum absolute difference 0), so `half_ok` and `err_ok` are unchanged: adding tubes per se adds no information.

**OA_runs** (200 rows = 2 parents × 10 designs × 10 allocations). The designs are OA_quadratic plus legal random, balanced random and annealed transversal (3 seeds each). Each of the 125 tubes takes one marker from each of five artificial families of five markers. The family constraint is a deterministic artificial slot constraint with no physical claim. Denominator: the 1,250 cross-family triples.

**Threshold_design** (120 rows = 2 datasets × 5 threshold offsets × 2 nodes × 2 designs × 3 allocations). Classical vs random design (seed 0) after shifting every marker threshold by −0.25 to +0.25, with the parent shares at each offset. Settings: Levine-32 T (r = 71 and 100; ε 0.06) and WINGMEN CD4 T (r = 130 and 180; ε 0.05). The offset-0 rows repeat Node_runs evaluations.

**Ablation_runs** (288 rows = 2 datasets × 2 parents × 2 nodes (r = 142, 180) × 3 initial replicates × 4 designs × 3 allocations). The same hybrid optimiser (optimiser seed 4, started from the classical stack) is run with only the requirement vector changed: uniform worst case, block-kNN imputation, or the LP audit of the initial split. The classical stack without optimisation is the baseline; requirement summaries are not applicable to it.

**Requirement_runs** (288 rows = 96 Levine-32 T + 192 WINGMEN CD4 T and CD8 T). Requirement vectors: the true LP requirement, the same values permuted across triples (3 shuffles) and the uniform worst case, each with optimiser seeds 4–6; plus the classical stack. Each is run for 2 initial replicates × 3 allocations.

**Ledger_runs** (216 rows; 54 with the true requirement). A second record of the e5i evaluations with the true and shuffled requirements (`half_ok` asserted identical). Columns: triples gained or lost on the half-width and error criteria relative to the classical stack, with their mean requirements and reference variances. `target_ledger_status` marks the 18 records whose per-target ledger was saved.

**Target_ledgers** (38,040 rows = 6 × 1,140 Levine-32 + 12 × 2,600 WINGMEN). The 18 saved per-target ledgers: true LP requirement, allocation 0, 3 settings × 2 initial replicates × 3 optimiser seeds. Only allocation 0 was saved, so target-level results for other allocations and for shuffles are not available; they were not reconstructed. `delta_lambda` = λ_opt − λ_base and the gained/lost flags are derived from the saved columns. Their counts reproduce the corresponding Ledger_runs rows exactly.

**Threshold_requirement** (192 rows = 2 datasets × 2 offsets (−0.25, +0.25) × 48). The requirement comparison of Requirement_runs, recomputed after shifting every threshold. Settings: Levine-32 T (ε 0.05, r = 142) and WINGMEN CD4 T (ε 0.03, r = 180), first initial design. The file stores neither ε, r nor the shuffle index: ε and r come from the frozen setting, and the shuffle index from the frozen row order. `zero_offset_eval_id` links each row to its zero-offset baseline in Requirement_runs (initial replicate 0, same requirement, optimiser seed and allocation). Shuffled requirements have no zero-offset counterpart because their permutation seeds differ.

**Planning_scale** (10 rows = 2 datasets × 5 parents) and **Planning_eps_used** (17 rows). For each parent, Planning_scale gives:

- the parent share and the total follow-up cells;
- the planning scale under an ideal balanced share and for the La Jolla covering (z = 1.96, p = 0.5);
- the follow-up cells needed for half-width 0.05 on every triple (total follow-up cells of all parents, not parent cells) and their ratio to the available cells;
- the replay tolerances actually used (none for the six parents without a design replay).

Planning_eps_used registers each replay tolerance with its module and source file.

## Supplementary Table S6

**Supplementary Table 6 | Calibration, self-checks, perturbation boundaries and real-batch reference (related to Figs. 3e, 4b, 4d and 4e and Supplementary Figs. 1e and 2d).**

Generated by `figures/scripts/table_S6.py` from frozen files by renaming, joins, parsing of stored strings and one unit conversion; no new test, interval or simulation was run. Proportions are on a 0–1 scale unless stated otherwise.

**Calibration** (40 rows). The saved synthetic calibration scenarios for the audit interval: 5 evidence structures (singles, pairs, pairs + direct, mixed, direct only) × 4 target frequencies (0, 0.01, 0.2, 0.5) × 2 cell numbers (600 and 6,000). Each row summarises 1,000 simulations; the individual simulations were not saved and are not reconstructed. `coverage` is the share of simulations whose audit interval (95%, Bonferroni within the claim) contains the true frequency, given with the stored exact 95% limits. An infeasible bound LP counts as not covering; none occurred. `mean_width` is the mean interval width.

**QC_runs** (5,600 rows = 2 datasets × (1,000 null + 9 × 200 perturbed allocations)). Every saved self-check allocation of the classical design (Levine-32 T, 71 tubes; WINGMEN CD4 T, 130 tubes), with the flags stored for each allocation. These flags use a separate Bonferroni family for each mode (single-marker or marker-pair checks; α = 0.001/M, where M is the number of checks testable in that mode) and are named `*_per_mode`. `single_pmin_bonf` and `pair_pmin_bonf` were stored as min(1, p_min·M), where p_min is the smallest p over the checkable tests of that mode. Values of 1 are therefore capped, so a minimum p above 1/M cannot be recovered; no p value is back-calculated or listed in this table. The scenario name "null" means no perturbation; it is not a missing value.

**QC_fixed_family** (20 rows = 2 datasets × 10 scenarios). The stored summaries that use one fixed family of r × 21 checks per allocation (α = 0.001/(r × 21); M = 1,491 for Levine-32 and 2,730 for WINGMEN). This is the definition behind the alarm rates in Fig. 4d and Supplementary Fig. 2d. The fixed-family alarm was recomputed after the run (`src/sum_e5d_fixed.py`) from the saved per-mode Bonferroni-adjusted minimum p values: each was divided by its mode's number of checkable tests to recover the minimum p, and the smaller of the two was then compared with the threshold of the fixed r × 21 family. A capped value gives 1/M, which lies above that threshold, so the cap changes no alarm. Alarm rates are given with the stored exact 95% intervals. Localisation and hit-given-alarm rates are not applicable for the null scenario or when no alarm occurred; status columns mark these cells. The fixed-family alarm and the per-mode flags of QC_runs are different definitions and are not interchangeable. As a consistency check, the table script re-derives the stored summaries from QC_runs in the same way (asserted). Null alarms: 0/1,000 (Levine-32) and 1/1,000 (WINGMEN).

**Boundary_runs** (280 rows = 2 datasets × (80 + 60)). Every saved perturbation replay, in two parts:

- Part A, unequal tube sizes: log-normal σ = 0, 0.25, 0.5 or 1; 10 replicates; classical vs random design.
- Part B, composition shift in 10% of the tubes: no shift, marker X measured in the tube, or X not measured; 20 replicates; classical design; with the fixed-family alarm.

Only the stored per-run values are given; no new interval was computed. Part B alarms: Levine-32 0/20 in each mode. WINGMEN 1/20 with no shift, 3/20 with X in the tube (all three at a shifted tube) and 0/20 with X not in the tube. The replay tolerances, 0.06 (Levine-32 T) and 0.05 (WINGMEN CD4 T), are chosen for displaying the mechanism; they are not the protocol ε.

**Batch_reference** (111 rows = 3 donors × 37 markers). Real batch differences in the CytoNorm example, where the same unstimulated control was acquired in two runs.
